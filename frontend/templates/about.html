About Hubgroup Systems

Our Story

Built on a Passion for Technology

Founded in 2016, Hubgroup Systems started as a small computer repair shop in Nairobi. Over the years, we grew into a comprehensive IT solutions provider, serving hundreds of businesses and individuals across Kenya.

Today, we operate offices in Nairobi and Mombasa, offering a wide range of services — from IT equipment sales and network infrastructure to printing, branding, and CCTV security systems. Our mission remains simple: deliver reliable, high-quality IT services that empower our clients.
